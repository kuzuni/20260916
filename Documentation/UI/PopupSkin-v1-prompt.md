## Runtime and limitations

Saved under Assets/DarkFantasyUI/Resources/Moonlit/Popup with unique metadata, preserved alpha, no mipmaps or NPOT scaling and max size 4096. All three PNGs were visually inspected and outer-corner alpha=0 confirmed without editing pixels.

PopupSkin creates cached Sprite objects at runtime. Panel border is 220 on each edge; button border is left/right 300 and top/bottom 210. Sliced rendering preserves corner regions when changing dimensions. Crimson close art is aspect-preserved, with a separate live ivory X label. Touch surfaces, labels and content icons remain independent. Panel raycast interception and existing modal-stack input ownership are retained.

Social, forge and progression module frames and action helpers share this skin; small colored data rows, equipment slots and main-screen art are unchanged. Selected/inactive tabs select separate blue/panel sprites. The center button flourish stretches with the middle rail and needs capture review at unusually wide/narrow sizes. This is closer themed artwork, not a pixel-exact reproduction. New cloud compilation, interaction regression and per-route visual captures are pending; do not claim all 24 screens accepted.
