# Gameplay update — 2026-09-18

## Latest six-skill direction

Only primitive and medieval skills are redesigned in this pass. Chicken/apple appear over the caster head, pulse larger/smaller exactly three times, then vanish as a green body aura begins and healing applies. Eight bones first surround the caster in a rotating circle, then sequentially fly to and swing against the enemy head. One rock travels in a parabola and bursts into stone fragments on contact. Five individual arrows follow sequential curved flights. One medieval sword appears and swings toward the enemy with a sword slash. Real damage/healing follows these visible contact moments; totals and turn cooldowns remain unchanged. Higher-era choreography remains as previously implemented.

Collection detail dialogs use a single 장착 button: keep an already equipped entry, fill an empty slot, or replace the weakest equipped entry when full. Numbered slot selection buttons are removed. Inspecting equipment never enables a green selection border; its rarity frame color remains.

Validation of this revision is pending cloud compilation, tests and actual capture review. New single bone/apple/arrow/sword art is generated with the built-in image tool; original PNG bytes and prompts are recorded under Documentation/Art.
